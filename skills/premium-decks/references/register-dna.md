# Register DNAs — consulting and banking

Two generic, unbranded design systems for the `consulting` and `banking`
registers. Their token values follow the corpus norms in
`../../../research/corpus-findings.md` (findings 8–9): a navy primary, 3–4
meaningful hues, and sans titles. `templates/lib/deck-kit.js` implements both.
The numbering continues from the seven product DNAs in `design-dna.md`, and
every rule for using a DNA there applies here too.

## 8 · Advisory Ink (consulting register — generic, from the corpus study)

White page, navy structure, one burnt-orange mark for the datum that carries
the title. This is not any firm's brand. The tokens follow the corpus norms:
navy primary, 3–4 meaningful hues, sans titles (`research/corpus-findings.md`,
findings 8–9).

- **Deck tokens:** bg `FFFFFF` · fg `1A1F2B` (16.5:1) · primary `1F3A5F`
  (11.5:1) · secondary text `5B6675` (5.8:1) · support `8FA3BF` · tint
  `E8EDF4` · accent `C2571A`. The accent is for marks and large figures only:
  4.49:1 is below the body-text threshold. RAG colours are used on status
  pages only: green `2E7D4F`, amber `C28A1A`, red `B3261E`.
- **Type (HTML):** IBM Plex Sans 600 titles, Plex Sans 400 body, Plex Mono for
  tables if needed. **Type (PPTX):** Arial Bold titles / Arial body (R9
  register override); title 22pt, body 12–14pt, notes and source 8–9pt.
- **Character:** tracker top left, whitespace (no rule) under the title, charts
  in navy and support greys, one accent mark per slide, generous left
  margin. The agenda is repeated with the current section in primary.
- **Guardrails:** one accent mark per slide. No shadows; depth comes from
  the tint fill. Navy is never a full-slide background except on the cover
  and dividers. Do not substitute a firm's signature colour for the primary.
- **Deck fit:** diagnostics, strategy recommendations, SteerCo updates,
  market studies.

## 9 · Board Ledger (banking register — generic, from the corpus study)

Deep navy and dark gold on white. The page is quiet: dense tables and neutral
titles. Gold marks the offer and the subject company. Navy primary, as in 30 of
48 coded board books (blue in 13 more).

- **Deck tokens:** bg `FFFFFF` · fg `111827` (17.7:1) · primary `0B2545`
  (15.4:1) · secondary `5B7089` (5.1:1) · rule `9AA8B8` · highlight-box tint
  `EDF1F6` · accent `8A6A00` (5.1:1) · subject-row tint `F5EEDC`.
- **Type (HTML):** Source Sans 3 600 titles and 400 body, tabular numerals
  throughout. **Type (PPTX):** Arial Bold titles 20pt / Arial body 10–12pt,
  notes 7–8pt. Dense tables are the norm here.
- **Character:** a navy title-only divider per analysis, a unit line under
  every numeric title, a numbered-notes block above the source line, and
  "Confidential" top right on the cover.
- **Guardrails:** gold marks only the offer line, the subject row, and the
  selected range. Tables use horizontal rules only. No icons or photography
  inside analysis pages. Negatives in parentheses.
- **Deck fit:** special-committee valuation books, fairness presentations,
  sell-side and strategic-alternatives books (`banking-pitchbook.md`).
